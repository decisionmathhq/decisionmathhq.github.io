Decision Math

Personal finance decisions, worked out with real numbers.

Decision Math is a YouTube channel that compares common money choices, such as HSA vs FSA or HDHP vs PPO, using transparent calculations and cited sources.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@decisionmathhq

About the Decision Math Uploader

The Decision Math Uploader is a private tool the channel owner uses to upload videos, thumbnails and captions to the Decision Math YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and only acts on the channel owner's own account.

Privacy policy · Videos are educational, not financial advice.
