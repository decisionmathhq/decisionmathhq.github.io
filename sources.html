How we work

Sources, updates & corrections

Every number in a Decision Math video or calculator comes from code that uses the figures below, never from memory. When a figure changes or we get something wrong, it is logged here.

The rules we follow

  • Numbers come from a calculator, and the web calculators are tested against the spreadsheet versions before they go up.
  • Every tax figure cites a primary source: the IRS, SSA or the survey that published it.
  • Simplifications are stated on screen and on each calculator page.
  • If a product is ever linked, it is only because a video's own numbers support it, and it is labeled as a paid link.
