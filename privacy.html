Privacy Policy

Decision Math Uploader · Last updated October 30, 2026

What this app is

The Decision Math Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Decision Math YouTube channel to upload that channel's own videos, thumbnails and captions through the YouTube Data API. It is not available to other users.

What data it accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app requests permission to upload videos and manage captions on the owner's YouTube channel. It uses that permission only to upload the owner's videos, set their thumbnails and add their captions, and to read basic channel details (such as the channel name) to confirm it is signed in to the correct channel.

How data is stored

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a file readable only by the owner. It is not sent to any server other than Google's. The app does not collect, sell, share or store any data about viewers or any other people.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It also follows the YouTube Terms of Service; see the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time from myaccount.google.com/permissions. Removing it stops the app from working and makes the stored token useless.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Decision Math channel at youtube.com/@decisionmathhq.
