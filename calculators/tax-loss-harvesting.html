How this calculator works

Losses first offset gains of the same type (short-term against short-term, long-term against long-term), then the other type. If you end with a net loss, up to $3,000 of it ($1,500 married filing separately) offsets ordinary income each year and the rest carries forward with no time limit. Harvested losses that offset long-term gains save tax at your capital gains rate (0%, 15% or 20%); losses that offset wages save at your ordinary bracket.

Later years assume the same wages and no other gains, under today's law, so they are an estimate.

Assumptions: federal only, wages are your only other income, standard deduction, no Net Investment Income Tax (3.8% above $200,000 single / $250,000 married MAGI), no state tax, no qualified dividends. Married filing separately is not modeled.
